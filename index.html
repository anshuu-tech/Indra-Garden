About Indra Garden

A Benchmark in Grand Weddings & Exclusive Celebrations

Situated on Bahar Unnao Gate Main Road near Naogoji Kua in Jhansi, Indra Garden provides an accommodating and hospitable setting for weddings, receptions, engagements, birthdays, and cultural gatherings. We combine an expansive outdoor lawn with covered facilities to ensure a comfortable celebration for you and your guests.

Open-Air & Covered Spaces

Weather-ready arrangements with broad covered dining and pavilion areas.

Bridal Dressing Facilities

Private preparation suites with attached washrooms for the family.

Continuous Power Supply

High-capacity generator backups running seamlessly throughout your event.

Convenient Access

Directly accessible via Bahar Unnao Gate road with ease for guest arrivals.
