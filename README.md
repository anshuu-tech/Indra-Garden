# 🌿 Indra Garden & Banquet | Jhansi

A premier, responsive website for **Indra Garden**, designed in the signature luxury resort aesthetic of **Gauri Greens** (clean white, lush emerald forest green `#2B5E27`, and gold/charcoal accents). Located at Bahar Unnao Gate, Near Naogoji Kua, Jhansi, Uttar Pradesh - 284002.

## 📁 Files
- `index.html` - Gauri Greens layout & structure with full responsiveness
- `style.css` - Custom clean scrollbar, date inputs, and FAQ accordion transitions
- `script.js` - WhatsApp inquiry routing, FAQ interactive accordions, and gallery filter
- `images/` - 12 authentic on-site photos of Indra Garden from Downloads (displayed strictly once in the gallery)

## 🌟 Key Features
- **Gauri Greens Inspired Theme:** Clean white background with forest green branding, Roboto & Playfair typography.
- **Top Utility & Contact Bar:** Immediate contact number, visiting hours, and WhatsApp link.
- **Why Indra Garden:** 6-feature highlight cards (Lush Green Property, In-House Parking, Best Decoration, Covered Catering, Dressing Suites, Hospitality).
- **The Venue & Spaces:** Descriptions of the Open Wedding Lawn, Covered Dining Pavilion, and Banquet & Suites.
- **Events & Add-Ons:** Interactive cards for weddings, sangeet, birthdays, anniversaries, and special setup features.
- **Exclusive Real Photo Gallery:** All 12 real photos from the user's laptop displayed in a clean 4-tab filterable gallery (zero duplicates).
- **Interactive FAQs:** Collapsible answers to common guest questions.
- **Enquire Now! Form:** Clean booking submission without pricing or size constraints, sending details directly to WhatsApp.
- **Integrated Google Maps:** Live location of Bahar Unnao Gate, Jhansi.
